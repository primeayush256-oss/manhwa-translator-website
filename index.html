The Flow

Three steps. Under a second.

No copy-pasting into another tab, no re-typing panel text into a translator. It happens exactly where you're already reading.

01 — Capture

Select the panel

Drag a box around any text — on a manhwa site, a manga reader, or a downloaded chapter. Works on anything visible in your browser.

02 — Translate

AI reads & translates

Gemini-powered OCR extracts the text, detects the source language automatically, and translates it in under a second.

03 — Read

Read it your way

Choose Hindi, English, or Hinglish. The translation appears in a floating overlay, right where the original text was.
